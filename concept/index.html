Method

A forecast is an argument, not an answer.

Calibra starts with goals, adds context, blends the two, and then shows the whole distribution. No single number is asked to carry the truth.

Read the methodology
01

Start with goals.

A Dixon–Coles baseline produces a full scoreline distribution from each side’s scoring and conceding rates.

02

Add context.

A boosted model reads form, rest, venue and rating context — the things a goals-only model cannot see.

03

Blend, then calibrate.

A fixed 60/40 blend keeps the scoreline shape coherent. Calibration then trims the confidence.

04

Show the uncertainty.

Every fixture keeps its full distribution. The headline is the most likely scoreline, not the only one.

Performance

We publish the misses, too.

The blend beats a goals-only baseline. It still trails the closing market. That gap is part of the story, not a footnote to hide.
